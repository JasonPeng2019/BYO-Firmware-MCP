## Notes

- D2 and C5 are marked "resolved as a consequence" per the implementer's directive: no
  code change was made specifically for them; they were closed by the FIX 1 and FIX 3(a)
  changes respectively, and are covered by those fixes' regression tests.
- D3 and D5 are the two findings ruled INVALID; both are left untouched in the code, as
  directed.
- FIX 8 required updating several existing tests' *expectations*, not just call sites,
  because the guide's step-0b "one canonical mint reproduces what all four former sites
  produced" invariant is necessarily superseded by provider-qualification (that is the
  fix). Specifically: `test_setup_overview_no_probe.py::ProbeConnectionIdTests` --
  `test_mint_is_stripped_and_casefolded` (asserted literal equality with the bare
  2-part legacy string) and `test_canonical_key_is_unchanged_versus_every_former_construction`
  (asserted `_setup_connection_key` produced the *same* key for the old and new mint)
  were rewritten to assert the new provider-qualified contract instead of the
  now-impossible old one; `test_stable_connection_identity_routes_through_the_shared_mint`'s
  mock metadata gained a `probe_family` field. Every other test in that class, plus
  mechanical call-site updates in `tests/test_probe_selection_records.py`,
  `tests/test_discovery_hook_workflow.py`, `tests/test_validation_honesty.py` (fixture
  handles needed a `probe_family`-bearing `board` so the provider check has something
  consistent to compare against), and `tests/test_server_trust_model_round_3.py` /
  `round_4.py` (hand-rolled legacy-shaped `connection_assignments` dicts updated to the
  canonical form `_setup_overview` now actually emits), only needed a provider argument
  or fixture field added -- their asserted *values* are unchanged.
